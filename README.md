# Zemi Echelon (`zemiechelon.com`)

The parent technology umbrella and digital portfolio representing the engineering systems, platforms, and products founded and architected by **Zubair Muwwakil**.

- **Domain:** [`zemiechelon.com`](https://zemiechelon.com)
- **Stack:** Next.js 16 (App Router) · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS v4 · Lucide React · Vercel

---

## Legal entities and independent SaaS products

**Zemi Echelon Inc.** is the intended commercial publisher of these independent brands:

| SaaS product | Domain | Purpose | Accounting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inunity** | [inunity.ca](https://inunity.ca) | Financial organization, records and forecasting | Independent product-level revenue and expenses under Zemi |
| **PB Social (The Pickleball Social)** | [pbsocial.ca](https://pbsocial.ca) | Sports operations, club subscriptions and player tools | Independent product-level revenue and expenses under Zemi |

Neither brand requires a separate legal corporation by default. Their customer data, subscriptions, contracts, product pricing and security access are kept distinct. Corporate IP assignments and public terms must be checked before legal publisher text is changed in production.

**Echelon Community Athletics Inc.** is a separate federally incorporated Canadian nonprofit that runs community athletic programs. It is **not** a subsidiary of Zemi. It may use PB Social under a duly approved service agreement. Its funds, governance, nonprofit discounts and programs remain separate.

## The Venture Ecosystem

### 1. Capital & Financial Operating Systems
- **[Inunity](https://inunity.ca)** (`github.com/zubairmuwwakil/MoneyTalks`): Personal finance command center with zero-bank-login Apple Pay transaction capture, multi-currency ledger, and 24 statutory compliance rules.
- **[PickMe](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/PickMe)**: 100% offline native iOS 18 card copilot for checkout reward maximization.
- **[MarketLens](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/marketdata)**: Enterprise Spring Boot 4 market data pipeline and technical indicator analytics service.
- **[Looply](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/return-saas)**: Autonomous email-derived commerce intelligence and subscription lifecycle tracker.

### 2. Autonomous Systems & Developer Platforms
- **[Agent Orchestrator](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/agent-orchestrator)**: Multi-agent runtime for distributed task delegation.
- **[Mindmap](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/mindmap)**: Visual system architecture and concept node graph.
- **[Glicko2-TS](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/glicko2-ts)**: Pure TypeScript rating engine for matchmaking.
- **[Command Quest](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/command-quest)**: Gamified CLI and shell mastery quest.

### 3. Sports & Operational Platforms
- **[The Pickleball Social / PickleOps](https://github.com/zubairmuwwakil/pickleops)**: Shipped iOS tournament & round-robin club operations engine.

### 4. Founder Laboratory
- **[Zubair Muwwakil Portfolio](https://zubairmuwwakil.com)**: Personal engineering portfolio and technical systems essays.

## Website discovery and product links

The existing immersive 3D atlas remains the homepage at `/`. A static, accessible
company overview at `/about/` explains the commercial software business, the
product ecosystem, and its founder, and provides normal HTML links for visitors
and search crawlers.

- `/about/`: company overview, product links, engineering work and founder
- `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt`: generated by the Next.js App Router
- Canonical domain: `https://zemiechelon.com`
- Product destinations: `https://pbsocial.ca`, `https://inunity.ca`; PickMe links
  to its GitHub development repository until it is publicly released
- Echelon Community Athletics Inc. is a *separate nonprofit*. Do not present it
  as a subsidiary, product, or an asset of Zemi Echelon.
- Confirm live product domains and legal publisher wording before merging or
  making reciprocal product-site changes.
